01 / About

Architecture is the art of making good decisions last.

I’m Somesh Jha, a technology leader and architect working at the intersection of business strategy, software architecture, cloud platforms, data, and applied AI.

My work is about finding the essential shape of a problem—then designing systems and operating models that teams can understand, build, and evolve. I bring an enterprise lens without losing sight of delivery: clear trade-offs, pragmatic roadmaps, and architecture that earns its keep in production.
