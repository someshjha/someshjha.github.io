The engagements

Four ways to start.

A compact view of duration, scope, and what you walk away with.

01 Decision sprint 02 Readiness review 03 AI readiness 04 Ongoing advisory
  1. 01 / Architecture Decision Sprint 1–2 weeks

    Architecture Decision Sprint

    For a consequential architecture decision where the organization needs an independent technical position.

    Typical situations: cloud or platform selection, modernization direction, build-vs-buy, distributed architecture, integration strategy, or an unresolved architecture disagreement between teams.

    • Architecture assessment
    • Options & trade-offs
    • Risk register
    • Recommendation
    • ADR
    • Executive readout
    Read: Building an architecture decision system ↗
  2. 02 / Architecture & Production Readiness Review 2–3 weeks

    Architecture & Production Readiness Review

    For a system approaching production, or one already experiencing scaling or reliability problems.

    Reviews architecture, security, data, resilience, observability, performance, deployment, and operational readiness across the system, then delivers findings ranked by severity with supporting evidence.

    • Findings
    • Severity ranking
    • Evidence
    • Remediation roadmap
  3. 03 / AI Production Readiness 2–4 weeks

    AI Production Readiness

    For an organization that has built an AI or agent proof of concept and now needs to answer: can we safely put this in production?

    Assesses evaluation, authorization, identity, tool access, data boundaries, human approval, observability, failure behaviour, and auditability against what production actually demands.

    • Evaluation
    • Authorization & identity
    • Tool & data boundaries
    • Human approval design
    • Observability
    • Auditability
    See evidence: Scoped agent access PoC ↗ Read: Choosing an agent framework ↗
  4. 04 / Strategic Architecture Advisory Ongoing

    Strategic Architecture Advisory

    For CTOs and VPs who need independent senior architecture support without adding another full-time executive.

    Ongoing coverage across architecture reviews, technology decisions, modernization roadmap, design escalation, engineering risk, and vendor evaluation—brought in as the questions arise, not on a fixed deliverable schedule.

    • Architecture reviews
    • Technology decisions
    • Modernization roadmap
    • Design escalation
    • Vendor evaluation
