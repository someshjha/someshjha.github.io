01 / About

Architecture is useful only when teams can deliver it.

I’m Somesh Jha. I lead senior architects and advise banking clients on the technology decisions that determine whether a platform actually reaches production.

Over the past 3.5 years I’ve grown the architecture and engineering practice I lead from roughly 3 to 54 people, operating through senior architects and distributed teams across banking, insurance, and telecom. That scale matters: knowing how to architect a distributed platform is one skill; knowing how 40+ engineers actually deliver it is another, and enterprise clients need both.

Across 13+ years of public work and generalized client engagements, I’ve led complex technology initiatives from discovery through delivery: modernizing monolithic applications, integrating mainframe systems with Kafka, defining cloud transformation roadmaps, and developing computer-vision and AI platforms.
